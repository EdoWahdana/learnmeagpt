## What the site covers

The site explains GPT-style language models: tokens, training versus use, the transformer calculation, and a GPT-2 forward pass.

## How a page is organized

The first paragraph defines the idea. A figure shows it. Calculation pages include a short Python block and, where listed, a browser tool.

## Which model is inspected

GPT-2 small has 12 layers, a hidden size of 768, 12 heads, a context length of 1024, and a vocabulary size of 50257. The weights are public. The explorer reads a committed trace of one sentence, not a live GPU.

## Which models are only described

Closed chat products, and later open architectures, are described on their own pages. Their weights are not loaded by this site in the explorer.

## What the site does not do

The site does not host accounts. It does not run a chat with a model. It does not accept private keys, passwords, or documents. The tools do not upload their input.
